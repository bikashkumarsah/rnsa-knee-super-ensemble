# Experiment Log

One row per run. Historical archive scores are context, not evidence for a new package. Our own
DINOv2 line peaked at **0.887** (run 4); from 2026-10-15 the champion candidate is the public-model
super-ensemble built by `super_ensemble/build_notebook.py`, submitted with the Kaggle CLI
(`super_ensemble/kaggle_flow.py`). A result is promoted only after its own completed Kaggle run scores
higher; the public split's SD is about 0.003, so 0.001-0.002 moves are noise.

Runs 1-4 used an OOF promotion gate (teacher macro AUC up with a non-negative report-group
bootstrap lower bound, gold-58 down by at most 0.01). The public members of the super-ensemble have
no OOF; gold-58 (`kaggle_flow.py push gold58`) is the only labelled sanity check, and Raptor's gold
numbers are optimistic because nartaa selected its checkpoints on them.
